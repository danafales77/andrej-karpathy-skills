Before you start

This tool keeps your Acquisition & Disposition record as a tamper-evident, hash-linked log — your electronic system of record (ATF variance on file). Nothing is edited in place; corrections are logged.

Back up regularly. Use Integrity → Download full backup to keep a current copy — that copy is your continuity and your surrender copy.

This is not legal advice. Confirm ATF requirements (27 CFR Part 478) and any state rules for your situation.

Export & print

The printout / PDF and the CSV are the human-readable copies of the record. Corrections travel with them: the original value stays visible, struck through, exactly as it appears in the book.

What to include

For page numbers, turn on Headers and footers in your browser's print dialog — a page counter is not something a web page can draw itself. Every page carries your licensee details and the record's head hash.

Record integrity

Every action is an entry in an append-only, hash-linked log. Editing or removing anything after the fact breaks the chain and is flagged here.

This log is your electronic system of record (ATF variance on file). Keep a current backup — it is your continuity copy and your surrender copy if the license ends.

A plain backup is your whole record in the clear — fine on a drive you control, not fine in a synced cloud folder. The encrypted one is safe to put anywhere; it is the same record under a passphrase only you hold. Lose the passphrase and the file is gone with it.

Backup policy

Alarm thresholds

These drive the warnings on the Inventory screen. They are your policy settings, not legal limits — every one approximates a rule with conditions this app does not model. Confirm the real thresholds for your situation and set them here.
